Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Overview

Gold Snipers ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our software, website, and related services.

2. Data Protection Principles

Gold Snipers is designed with privacy and data minimization in mind. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, trading history, or signal channel configurations to third-party marketers.

3. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

  • Purchase information: Name, email address, and billing details provided to our payment processor (Stripe) during checkout. We do not store payment card numbers directly.
  • Account & License information: License key, email address, and connected MT5 account identifiers for authentication and service delivery.
  • Website analytics: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for maintaining security and improving the website.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with trusted third-party providers that process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe: Payment processing, subscription management, and tax compliance.
  • MetaAPI: Cloud bridge infrastructure connecting to MetaTrader 5 broker servers.
  • Tawk.to: Optional live support chat widget on our website.

5. How Your Data Is Used

  • To process your subscription and deliver your license access
  • To authenticate your account and enforce plan limits
  • To provide customer support when you contact us
  • To send important service updates (you may opt out of non-essential communications)

6. Data Storage & Security

Purchase and license data is stored using encrypted database infrastructure. We retain your account information for as long as your license or subscription remains active, and implement industry-standard security controls to safeguard your data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to processing of your data or request data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via Telegram Support.

8. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for session preferences and consent state. Tawk.to may set session cookies if you accept live chat cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.
