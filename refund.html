Refund Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on your initial paid monthly subscription ($49/month Gold Snipers Cloud). If you are not satisfied within the first 7 days, we will refund your software payment in full. Broker-sponsored (Deposit & Trade) accounts carry a $0 upfront software fee.

1. Eligibility

You may request a software subscription refund if:

  • Your request is submitted within 7 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase date.
  • You subscribed directly through Gold Snipers (goldsnipersbot.com).

2. How to Request a Refund

  • Contact us via Telegram Support with your checkout email address.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days back to your original payment method.

3. Subscription Cancellation ($49/mo)

You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time with one click through your self-service Stripe billing portal to prevent any future monthly charges.

4. Broker-Sponsored Accounts (Deposit & Trade — $0 Upfront)

If you access Gold Snipers through our official partner broker (TradeQuo) via the Deposit & Trade option:

  • No Software Fee to Refund: Because broker-sponsored accounts carry a $0 upfront software fee, there is no purchase transaction with Gold Snipers to refund.
  • Your Capital Remains 100% Yours: Your deposited trading capital is held directly by our partner broker (TradeQuo) in your own segregated account. Gold Snipers never holds or manages your funds. You may withdraw your available balance directly from your broker at any time.
  • Trading Losses Are Non-Refundable: Gold Snipers is an automated execution software tool, not an asset manager. We cannot refund market trading losses.

5. Exceptions

Refund requests submitted more than 7 calendar days after the initial purchase date are outside the refund window. Third-party connectivity fees paid directly to MetaAPI are governed by MetaAPI's billing terms.
