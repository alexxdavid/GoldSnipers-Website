Last updated: September 21, 2026

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid license purchases (Monthly & Lifetime). If you're not satisfied, we'll refund your software payment — no questions asked. Broker-sponsored (Deposit & Trade) accounts carry $0 upfront software fee.

Eligibility

You may request a refund if:

  • Your refund request is made within 7 calendar days of your original purchase date.
  • You purchased directly from Gold Snipers (goldsnipersbot.com or an authorized reseller).

How to Request a Refund

  • Contact us via Telegram Support with your purchase email and order reference.
  • We will process your refund within 5-10 business days.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.

What Happens After a Refund

  • Your license key will be deactivated and you will lose access to the Software.

Lifetime Plans

Lifetime plans are eligible for a refund within 7 days of purchase. After 7 days, lifetime purchases are non-refundable as you have received perpetual access to the Software and all future updates.
