Our Architecture

Four foundational principles

Every part of Gold Snipers Cloud is designed for reliability, clarity, and capital preservation.

Pillar 01

100% Cloud-Native Execution

No desktop installers, no DLL files, and no VPS servers to maintain. Gold Snipers Cloud runs 24/7 in high-availability datacenters and bridges directly to your MT5 broker via MetaAPI.

Pillar 02

Dynamic Grid Ladder Engine

Instead of a single flat market entry, our engine can split signals into an immediate Anchor order plus a multi-tier Pending Limit Ladder across the entry zone to improve your average fill price.

Pillar 03

Automated Profit Shaving & Break-Even

When TP1 or subsequent targets hit, the cloud automatically shaves off your configured volume percentage and shifts your Stop Loss to break-even or trailing profit lock.

Pillar 04

Complete Feature Equality

No feature gating or confusing add-ons. Whether you join via our $0 upfront partner broker sponsorship or the $49/month multi-broker cloud plan, you get the complete suite.
