Connecting Your MetaTrader 5 (MT5) Broker

Gold Snipers Cloud connects directly to your broker through MetaAPI. Once signed into the Cloud Web App:

  • Open the Accounts tab in your dashboard.
  • Enter your MetaAPI token and click Connect Account.
  • Select your broker server and enter your MT5 account login & password.
  • Your account connects in seconds and stays synchronized 24/7 in the cloud.
