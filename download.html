Connecting Your MT4 / MT5 Broker

Gold Snipers Cloud communicates with your broker via a direct MetaAPI connection. Once you sign in to the Cloud Web App:

  • Navigate to the Accounts tab in your dashboard.
  • Enter your MetaAPI token and click Connect Account.
  • Select your broker server, enter your MT4/MT5 login & investor/master password.
  • Your account will connect in seconds and stay synchronized 24/7 without requiring any local terminal.
